Analyse the endpoint as an Application Security Engineer.

1. Identify potential vulnerabilities.
2. Explain the causes and the impact.
3. Classify them according to the applicable OWASP categories.
4. Propose a fixed implementation.
5. Generate tests for authorised and unauthorised access.
6. Define how to validate the fix.

Do not assume that authentication implies authorisation.
Do not invent access rules.
Distinguish inferred results from tested ones.
